# Consumer Research & Marketing Analysis

## Overview

A recurring theme in my university and early research work was taking qualitative or survey information and turning it into structured marketing insight.

The private archive includes interview transcripts, coded relationship matrices, market-survey files, advertising briefs, brand-management work, and research/report-writing assignments.

## Depth interviews and concept mapping

In one consumer-behavior exercise, I conducted long-form interviews and probed factors such as:

- brand preference,
- price,
- taste,
- perceived quality,
- health perceptions,
- mood,
- socializing,
- ambience,
- nostalgia,
- satisfaction,
- and repeat consumption.

I then worked with coded concepts and a relationship matrix to connect product attributes to emotional and behavioral outcomes.

This was an early version of a pattern I still use: **ask better questions → structure messy information → identify relationships → explain what matters for a decision.**

## Other surviving work

The archive also includes examples such as:

- a Lipton advertising brief with audience insight, positioning, brand persona, brand promise, and measurable objectives,
- a brand-management group project,
- market-survey analysis,
- content/research briefs,
- and presentations/reports I created for other students.
