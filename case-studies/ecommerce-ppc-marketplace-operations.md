# E-commerce PPC & Marketplace Operations

## Overview

My e-commerce experience developed in two stages: first through structured marketplace work and training at Sellercoop, then through founder/operator experience at Retail Medley.

The useful part of that progression is that I moved from **planning campaigns on paper** to **working inside Seller Central and running marketplace decisions myself**.

## Sellercoop — learning the operating discipline

In my early Sellercoop work, PPC planning was intentionally manual before account access.

I prepared campaign structures in spreadsheets, researched products and keywords, and presented the proposed setup before being given access to the live Seller Central environment.

That process taught me to think about:

- keyword intent,
- campaign/ad-group structure,
- product economics,
- listing quality,
- marketplace competition,
- spend discipline,
- and what a client needs to understand before a campaign goes live.

The private archive still contains historical Amazon task sheets, product-research material, keyword files, PPC planning documents, and marketplace analysis.

## Retail Medley — founder-side execution

Later, as a co-founder/operator at Retail Medley, I worked with my own Seller Central activity rather than only preparing recommendations for someone else's account.

My work included combinations of:

- PPC campaign planning and execution,
- product and keyword research,
- listing optimization,
- supplier coordination,
- inventory/availability thinking,
- marketplace operations,
- and commercial decision-making.

## Broader digital measurement exposure

I also have hands-on exposure to Google Ads and Google Analytics, which complements the marketplace work with a broader digital-advertising and measurement perspective.
